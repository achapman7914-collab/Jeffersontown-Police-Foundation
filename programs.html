Programs We Support

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation helps strengthen established Jeffersontown Police Department programs that care for officers, support victims and families, and connect members of our community with meaningful resources.

Foundation support helps existing JPD programs go further by providing resources, opportunities and flexible assistance beyond traditional public-safety funding.
