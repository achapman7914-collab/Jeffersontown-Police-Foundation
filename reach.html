The REACH Unit of the Jeffersontown Police Department provides compassionate, professional social-services support to individuals and families facing crisis. REACH personnel work alongside officers and community partners to remove barriers, connect people to resources and support movement toward safety, recovery and stability.

What Is REACH?

REACH—Respond, Evaluate, Advocate, Connect and Heal—is an embedded social-services program within the Jeffersontown Police Department supporting victims of crime and individuals experiencing substance use, mental-health, homelessness and other complex needs.

REACH in the Community

Working alongside officers and connecting in the community are central to the REACH approach.

Real Impact. Real Change.

Connection Can Change What Happens Next.

One early Angel Outreach case involved a young adult who had experienced repeated accidental drug poisonings and emergency-service calls. REACH personnel conducted four home visits, supported the family, provided harm-reduction education, accompanied the individual into treatment and connected the individual with additional recovery and vocational resources.

Program follow-up has found no new accidental drug poisonings or calls for service for more than three years—and counting—since those services were provided.

The REACH Process

●

Respond

REACH personnel meet people where they are when a need, crisis or referral is identified.

✓

Evaluate

REACH personnel listen, assess needs and identify concerns, strengths and appropriate next steps.

◆

Advocate

REACH helps individuals and families navigate systems, rights, services and barriers.

↔

Connect

REACH connects people with mutually agreed-upon treatment, resources and community supports.

♥

Heal

REACH provides continued support as individuals and families move toward safety, recovery and stability.

REACH History

2015

JPD began researching alternative responses to drug use and arrest during the opioid crisis.

2016

JPD became Kentucky's first Angel Program and the 100th partner of PAARI.

2020

Embedded social work and Victim Services expanded JPD's civilian support model.

2022

Victim Services, Angel Outreach and additional social-service pathways came together under the REACH model.

Today

REACH continues to expand connections, partnerships and support throughout Jeffersontown.

Former Major Brittany Garrett

Her leadership in developing JPD's Angel Program helped establish the roots of what would later grow into the REACH model.
