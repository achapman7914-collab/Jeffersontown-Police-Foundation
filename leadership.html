Jeffersontown Police Foundation

Board of Directors

Community leaders united by a shared mission to strengthen public safety, support the Jeffersontown Police Department and invest in the people and programs that serve Jeffersontown.

Executive Officers
President

Shellie May

Executive Director, Office of Senior Protection & Mediation • Kentucky Office of the Attorney General

Shellie brings experience in city and state government, grant writing and reporting, strategic planning, fundraising and community partnerships. Her background includes service with Jeffersontown City Government and strong relationships across the community and business sector.

Vice President

Connor F. Cogan

Assistant Manager of Construction • Jefferson Development Group

Connor brings business leadership, fundraising experience and deep local connections through his family's long-standing development business in Jeffersontown. He is focused on building relationships with businesses and community partners to advance the Foundation's mission.

Treasurer

P. Darin Epperly

Contracting & Consulting

Darin's career spans customer service, insurance, finance and mortgages, construction and restoration, sales, relationship building, event planning and fundraising. He brings broad experience in finance, nonprofit governance, community engagement and strategic planning.

Secretary

Judy Garza

Retired Respiratory Therapist

Judy's professional background is in respiratory therapy, including in-home ventilator management, with strengths in education and customer service. She also brings experience in fundraising, community engagement, strategic planning and communications.

Board Members
Board Member

Mike Mason

President • The LawnPro

Mike has spent roughly three decades building a multi-million-dollar landscape maintenance company. His nonprofit leadership includes service on the Snow and Ice Management Association board and as a founding member and Vice Chair of the SIMA Foundation.

Board Member

Brad Carson

Owner • J-Town Hardware & Rental

Brad brings a successful sales and business-leadership career, local business ownership and strong Jeffersontown connections. His leadership experience includes service with the Jeffersontown Chamber and the University of Toledo School of Business board.

Board Member

Deidra Henley Sanders

Co-Owner • Clinical Forensic Nurse Consultants, LLC

Deidra brings approximately 50 years of healthcare experience as an RN and APRN, along with experience in nursing leadership, education, private practice and medical science. She also served for 17 years as a SANE nurse and forensic nurse examiner.

Board Member

Bryan K. Wimsatt

Developer / Builder / Manager • MRC Development Group, LLC

Bryan brings extensive experience in commercial and residential development, project management, financing and budgeting. His background also includes board service and business leadership within the Jeffersontown community.

Board Member / Legal Counsel

Gena McCubbins

Owner & Sole Practitioner • Gena McCubbins Law

Gena brings legal, financial and nonprofit experience to the Foundation. Before opening her own practice, she served as COO for a local nonprofit and worked with the FBI's Citizens Academy, giving her insight into nonprofit operations and law-enforcement/community relationships.

Board Member

Amy Hieb

Senior Manager, Organizational Development & Change Management • Yum! Brands, Inc.

Amy brings more than 20 years of human-resources experience, along with strengths in organizational development, strategic planning, fundraising and nonprofit governance. Her prior nonprofit leadership includes service as a board member and later Chairman of Safe Passage.
